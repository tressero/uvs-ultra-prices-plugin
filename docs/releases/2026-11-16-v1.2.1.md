# v1.2.1 — a game-card icon that pops

## Change

- **New icon.** The blue card stack is replaced by a night-navy tile, an orange
  card behind a white card, and a glowing orange price tilted with the card. The
  front card gets a holo-foil streak, a hot rim light and small sparks, so it
  reads like a trading card from a game rather than a generic dollar sign.
- **UVS wordmark.** The front card carries a small `UVS` under the price at
  128px, like a card nameplate. It is left off at 48px and below, where it would
  turn into a smudge.
- **Toolbar fallback.** At 16px the cards and effects are dropped and the icon
  keeps the bright orange dollar sign on the same dark tile, so it stays legible
  in the Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox toolbars.

The icon is generated from SVG by `npm run icons`; every size is rendered from
the same source. Store-listing icons may need to be re-uploaded in the Chrome Web
Store and AMO dashboards after this release.

## Upgrade note

No new permissions and no behavior changes. The popup, deck, search and list
pages are untouched.
