# v1.2.0 — a deck row shows what all its copies cost

## Change

- **Card prices on a deck list are now line totals.** Until now every row showed
  the price of a single copy, so a playset of four $15 cards read as `$15.00`
  even though the row costs $60. Each row now multiplies the price by its
  quantity: four copies at $15 show `$60.00`. Hovering the price still shows the
  unit price as `4 × $15.00`, plus the mid, market and low alternatives.
- **Estimates multiply the same way.** A row of four unpriced ultra rares shows
  `$60 (Est.)` instead of `$15 (Est.)`, so the row and the deck total agree.

Search results and card pages carry one copy each, so their chips are unchanged.

## Upgrade note

No new permissions and no settings change. The deck total at the top of the page
was already quantity-aware, so it stays the same.
