# v1.1.0 — prices everywhere, safer Mass Entry

## Highlights

- TCGplayer prices now appear on card search results and card pages, including the
  popup opened from a deck row.
- **Buy deck on TCGplayer** sends only cards with a real TCGplayer price. A dialog
  lists the cards that will be left out and why before Mass Entry opens.
- Price basis is selectable: **Mid** (listed median), **Market** (recent sold
  prices, default) and **Low** (cheapest listing). The extension popup sets the
  default; a deck page can override it for that page only.
- Estimates are shorter and clearer: `$1 (Est.)`, `$15 (Est.)`, `$30 (Est.)`.
- The popup shows the build (`v1.1.0.<sha>`) linked to its commit on GitHub.
- Releases publish `dist-<label>.zip` for manual install and the Chrome Web Store.

## Upgrade note

The daily price snapshot now stores the listed median (`midPrice`). The worker is
deployed as part of the release; until it is, the Mid option falls back to market
prices.
